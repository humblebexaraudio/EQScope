EQSCOPE by HUMBLE BEXAR AUDIO
=============================

EQScope shows exactly what an EQ plug-in does. It sends a perfectly flat test
signal into the EQ and draws the EQ's frequency and phase response live, as you
turn its knobs.

This installs:
  - the VST3 plug-in, in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
    (Ableton Live, Cubase, Reaper, Studio One, Bitwig, FL Studio...)
  - the standalone app (optional)
  - the user guide, with Start menu shortcuts

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

If EQ Probe (EQScope's earlier name) is installed, this replaces it. Sessions
saved with EQ Probe open in EQScope with their settings.

Close your DAW before continuing.
