HUMBLE BEXAR AUDIO

EQScope is installed

  1. Restart your DAW so it finds EQScope.
  2. In Logic, if it doesn't appear: Logic Pro → Settings → Plug-in Manager, select EQScope, click Reset & Rescan Selection.
  3. Put EQScope (SIGNAL) → your EQ → EQScope (ANALYZE) on one channel and open the last one.

The user guide is in /Applications/Humble Bexar Audio/EQScope User Guide.pdf. The plug-in also has short instructions built in: press ? INSTRUCTIONS on the faceplate.
