HUMBLE BEXAR AUDIO

Welcome to EQScope

EQScope shows exactly what an EQ plug-in does. It sends a perfectly flat test signal into the EQ and draws the EQ's frequency and phase response live, as you turn its knobs.

This installs:

  • Audio Unit for Logic Pro, GarageBand and MainStage
  • VST3 for Ableton Live, Cubase, Reaper, Studio One, Bitwig and FL Studio
  • The standalone app and the user guide, in /Applications/Humble Bexar Audio

Click Customize on the Installation Type step to leave any of these out. Requires macOS 12.7 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

If EQ Probe (EQScope's earlier name) is installed, this replaces it. Sessions saved with EQ Probe open in EQScope with their settings.
