EQSCOPE IS INSTALLED
====================

1. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins if it doesn't do so by itself.
   EQScope is listed under Humble Bexar Audio.

2. On one track, insert:
      EQScope (press SIGNAL)  ->  the EQ you want to see  ->  EQScope (ANALYZE)
   Use a track that is processing (some DAWs only run plug-ins while playing,
   or while a track is armed or monitoring).

3. Open the ANALYZE copy. When the green LOCKED light is on, the curve is
   exact. Turn the EQ and the curve follows within about 0.18 seconds.

For very narrow bands below about 60 Hz, press FINE on the SIGNAL copy.

The user guide is in the Start menu under Humble Bexar Audio. The plug-in also
has short instructions built in: press ? INSTRUCTIONS on the faceplate.
