Before you start

How EQScope is used

You use two copies of EQScope on one channel, with the EQ you want to see between them:

EQScope (SIGNAL) → your EQ → EQScope (ANALYZE)

Open the ANALYZE copy. When the green LOCKED light is on, the curve is exact. Turn your EQ and the curve follows within about 0.18 seconds.

In Logic Pro

Use an Aux channel strip (Mixer → Options → Create New Auxiliary Channel Strip). Aux strips are always live, so you don't need to press play. EQScope is under Audio FX → Audio Units → Humble Bexar Audio.

Good to know

  • The SIGNAL copy outputs a test signal at −18 dBFS. The ANALYZE copy is silent unless you turn on MONITOR.
  • For very narrow bands below about 60 Hz, press FINE on the SIGNAL copy.
  • Choose a faceplate finish with the FINISH swatches at the bottom left.
